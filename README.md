# 💸 NoMoney - Your Smart Personal Finance Companion

**NoMoney** is a cutting-edge personal finance app powered by Alibaba's Model Studio. Designed to help individuals manage their finances effortlessly, NoMoney combines intelligent budgeting, receipt scanning, and deep AI-driven financial insights — all in one sleek app.

## 🚀 Features

### 🧠 AI Financial Assistant - *NoMoney Bot*
Meet **NoMoney Bot**, your smart finance advisor. Built on Qwen and Alibaba’s large language models, it analyzes your financial behavior and offers personalized recommendations to help you make smarter money decisions.

### 📸 Intelligent Receipt Scanning (OCR + Qwen)
Say goodbye to manual data entry. With powerful OCR and AI integration, NoMoney automatically scans, reads, and categorizes receipts, turning paper clutter into clean digital records.

### 💼 Smart Budgeting
Create flexible budgets, monitor your spending in real-time, and receive proactive alerts when you’re nearing your limits. Stay in control of your money — every step of the way.

### 📊 Deep Financial Insights
NoMoney learns from your income and spending patterns to provide visual reports, actionable insights, and even savings strategies tailored to your financial goals.

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- **Alibaba Model Studio**
- **Qwen Large Language Model**
- **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)**
- **Cross-platform Frontend (Flutter / React Native / etc.)**
- **Secure Cloud Storage**

## 🛡️ Privacy First
Your financial data stays yours. We ensure bank-grade encryption and follow best practices to protect your privacy.

## 📈 Why NoMoney?
Whether you’re a student trying to manage allowance, a freelancer juggling irregular income, or simply someone who wants to be more financially mindful — **NoMoney** gives you clarity and confidence over your finances.
